When performing a DFS traversal, it can be useful finding backedges, i.e edges to nodes we would have visited, had we not done so already. A simple way of doing this, is to mutate your undirected graph to a directed graph during the DFS traversal.

 1class Graph:
 2    def __init__(self, adjlist):
 3        self.visited = set()
 4        self.adjlist = adjlist
 5
 6    def dfs(self, parent, node):
 7        if node in self.visited:
 8            print(f"backedge: {parent} -> {node}")
 9            return
10        print(f"{node}")
11        self.visited.add(node)
12        for neighbour in self.adjlist[node]:
13            self.adjlist[neighbour].remove(node)
14            self.dfs(node, neighbour)
